## What is FSR?

"FSR (Faculty Study Report) is a comprehensive performance evaluation document that summarizes a faculty member's activities including:
- Teaching load and schedules
- Research projects and publications
- Extension/community service activities
- Administrative work
- Professional development
- Awards and recognitions

It's typically required for performance reviews, promotions, and tenure applications."

## Generating Your FSR

### For Faculty Members
"To generate your FSR:
1. Go to your Member Dashboard
2. Scroll to the 'FSR Generation' section
3. Select the time period (semester and school year)
4. Click 'Generate FSR'
5. Wait for processing (usually 10-30 seconds)
6. Download the generated Excel file

The FSR will automatically include all your:
- Teaching schedules from that period
- Submitted research projects
- Extension activities
- Any administrative roles"

### For Admins
"Admins can generate FSR for any faculty member:
1. Go to the 'FSR' page from the admin sidebar
2. Select a faculty member from the dropdown
3. Choose the time period
4. Click 'Generate FSR for [Name]'
5. Download or view the generated report

This is useful for performance reviews or when a faculty member needs assistance."

## FSR Contents

### Teaching Section
- Complete class schedule for the period
- Total teaching hours per week
- Number of courses taught
- Number of students (if available)
- Co-teaching arrangements

### Research Section
- Published papers and journal articles
- Research projects (ongoing and completed)
- Conference presentations
- Research grants received
- Collaborations and partnerships

### Extension Section
- Community outreach programs
- Extension projects
- Training/seminars conducted
- Community beneficiaries served
- Extension hours contributed

### Administrative Section
- Committee memberships
- Administrative positions held
- Leadership roles
- Institutional service hours

## FSR Format

"The FSR is generated as an Excel (.xlsx) file following the standard UPLB CHE format. It includes:
- Cover page with faculty information
- Detailed breakdowns by category
- Summary statistics
- Data sourced from the CERP system

You can further edit the Excel file if needed before submission."

## Important Notes

### Data Accuracy
"The FSR pulls data directly from the CERP system. To ensure accuracy:
- Keep your research submissions up to date
- Submit extension activities promptly
- Verify your teaching schedule is correct
- Update any administrative roles

The FSR is only as accurate as the data in the system."

### Audit Trail
"All FSR generation activities are logged in the system audit trail. Admins can view:
- Who generated an FSR
- When it was generated
- For which faculty member
- Time period covered

This provides accountability and tracking."

### Deadlines
"Check with your department for FSR submission deadlines, typically:
- End of semester for semester reports
- End of academic year for annual reports
- As required for promotion/tenure applications

Generate your FSR early to allow time for review and corrections."

## Troubleshooting

### Missing Data
"If your FSR is missing expected information:
1. Check that you've submitted all research/extensions in the system
2. Verify the correct time period is selected
3. Ensure your schedule data exists for that semester
4. Contact admin if data appears incorrect"

### Download Issues
"If the FSR won't download:
1. Check your browser's download settings
2. Ensure pop-ups are not blocked
3. Try generating again (it may have timed out)
4. Contact IT support if the issue persists"

### Format Issues
"If the FSR format looks incorrect:
1. Open with Microsoft Excel (not Google Sheets)
2. Enable editing if the file opens in protected mode
3. Check that macros are not blocked
4. Use the reference FSR template for comparison"
