# CERP 2.0 System Information

## What is CERP?
CERP stands for Center for Extension and Research in the Philippines. CERP 2.0 is an AI-powered faculty management and class scheduling system for the College of Human Ecology (CHE) at UPLB (University of the Philippines Los Baños).

## System Features
- **Faculty Management**: Track faculty members, their roles, and activities
- **Class Scheduling**: Generate optimal schedules using genetic algorithms
- **Research Management**: Submit and track research projects
- **Extension Programs**: Manage community outreach and extension activities
- **FSR Generation**: Faculty Study Reports for performance evaluation
- **TAP/HSP**: Technical Assistance Program and Health Services Program tracking
- **News & Events**: Announcements and calendar management

## User Roles
- **Admin**: Full access to scheduling, member management, reports
- **Faculty/Member**: View own schedules, submit research/extensions, generate FSR

## Key Terms
- **FSR**: Faculty Study Report - performance evaluation document
- **GA**: Genetic Algorithm - optimization method for scheduling
- **CHE**: College of Human Ecology at UPLB
- **Section**: Student group/class (e.g., Section A, B)
- **Semester**: 1st or 2nd semester of academic year
- **School Year**: Academic year format (e.g., 2026-2027)
