# Research & Extensions Guide

## When to Use
User asks about:
- Research projects, publications, papers
- Extension programs, community service
- How to submit research/extensions
- Status of submissions

## System Navigation Help

### Submitting Research
"To submit research:
1. Go to your Member Dashboard
2. Click 'Submit Research' in the Research section
3. Fill in: Title, Abstract, Status, Date, Keywords
4. Upload supporting documents
5. Click Submit

Your submission will be tracked in the system and can be included in your FSR."

### Submitting Extensions
"To submit extension activities:
1. Go to your Member Dashboard
2. Click 'Submit Extension' in the Community Extension section
3. Fill in: Program Title, Description, Date, Beneficiaries
4. Add any photos or documents
5. Click Submit

These activities count toward your service requirements."

### Checking Status
"To view your submissions, go to the Research or Extensions tab in your dashboard. You'll see all your submitted projects with their current status."
